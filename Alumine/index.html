About Us

At Alumine India, we are dedicated to pushing the boundaries of design and functionality. We specialize in providing innovative architectural solutions that transform spaces and inspire creativity. Whether you're constructing a new building, renovating an existing structure, or seeking to enhance the aesthetics and efficiency of your environment, our comprehensive range of services and products are designed flawlessly.

The valuable reltionships we have built with our customers thorugh teamwork and partnering are the cornerstones of our success. We share a strong sense of purpuse and commitment to “getting it right the frist time.

Our Services

Upvc Fins

Our uPVC fins offer a versatile solution for shading, privacy, and aesthetic enhancement. Designed for durability and low maintenance, our fins are customizable to match any design and are perfect for new constructions and renovations alike.
